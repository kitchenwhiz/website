Questions

Cloud, QSR & central kitchens: common questions

Can you plan a central kitchen that supplies several outlets?

Yes. We design the hub kitchen for volume and the outlets for finishing and holding, so each outlet needs only a small footprint. Our central kitchen clients include Akshaya Patra, MTR and ITC.

Do you make equipment to custom sizes?

Yes. Work tables, burner ranges, sinks, racks, hoods and counters are fabricated in our Bommasandra factory in SS202 or SS304, to the exact sizes of your site.

Can we see the equipment before we buy?

Yes. Our 20,000 sq ft showroom and demo centre in Bommasandra has Indian and imported equipment on display, and your chef can try it in a live demo. Call ahead for a demo slot.

Do you install exhaust and fire suppression too?

Yes. Installation covers placement, exhaust hoods, ducting and fire suppression, and we commission the kitchen on site before handing over.

What should I send to get a quote?

Your floor plan or site photos, the cuisine, and the number of covers or orders per day. Email info@kitchenwhiz.in or WhatsApp 99020 14700.
