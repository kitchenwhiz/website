About HeatKO

What we make

From dimsum steamers and robata grills to IR strip heaters, HeatKO equipment is built for the demands of professional kitchens.

Each unit is designed to keep the electrical load low while staying safe and durable, with a clean, functional finish that looks good on an open line.

HeatKO IR heaters also fit the overhead shelves of our USP Ecoline work tables, to keep food warm at the pass.

The HeatKO range

  • Dimsum steamer
  • Cheung fun steamer
  • Pasta blancher
  • Robatayaki / sigadi charcoal griller
  • Hot bain marie
  • IR strip heaters (IR500, IR800)
