What to send us

The more we know up front, the faster we can come back with a layout and an equipment list.

  • Floor plan or a sketch with dimensions
  • Photos or a video of the site
  • Cuisine and menu style
  • Covers per day, or orders per hour for delivery
  • Gas, power and water points if already fixed
  • Your budget range and opening date
