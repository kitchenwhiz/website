Our story

From an SJP Road shop to living kitchens

KitchenWhiz is the commercial kitchen brand of Unique Steel Products, a Bengaluru family business now in its second generation.

Factory 10k
Showroom 10k
Warehouse 15k

How our 35,000 sq ft facility in Bommasandra is split.

Shabbir Madraswala started Trade Links Corporation in 1977 from a small shop on SJP Road, trading hardware, tools and safety products. Shireen Madraswala later took charge of sales and customer relationships, and built the trust the business still runs on.

Today a team of close to 100 people covers design, projects, sales, manufacturing and service. Our aim is simple: to stay at the forefront of commercial kitchens, and be an invaluable part of our clients' living kitchens.

  • 1977Trade Links CorporationA small trading shop on SJP Road, Bengaluru.
  • 1988Into manufacturingA move to an industrial shed in Bommasandra to build stainless-steel stoves, then commercial kitchen equipment. KitchenWhiz is born.
  • 2003The next generationHuzefa Madraswala joins with an industrial engineering background and brings in new systems. He leads the company today.
  • TodayFour brands and the UNOX LoungeKitchenWhiz, Barwhiz, HeatKO and USP, plus Bengaluru's UNOX Lounge, all under one roof.

Every format, every cuisine

Restaurants & hotels

Pan Asian, continental, Indian, Mediterranean and more. From compact to large spaces, designed for efficiency with an eye on aesthetics, modular and tailor-made to any space.

Bakeries

From mass production, high-end restaurants and microbreweries to artisanal and home bakers. Combi ovens now keep every batch consistent.

Equipment: Ovens, mixers, sheeters, slicers, proofers, refrigeration, tables and racks.

Clients include: ITC Sunfeast, Lavonne, Thom's Bakery, Glen's Bakehouse, Milano, Loafer & Co.

Pizzerias

Customised wood-fired ovens, prep counters and coffee machines that complete the Italian feel.

Equipment: Wood-fired and conventional ovens, kneaders, refrigeration, display counters, wine chillers.

Clients include: Brik Oven, Holy Doh, Thin Tony, Go Pizza

Cafés

Front-of-house food display with tabletop cooking and dispensing equipment. Speed ovens give fast turnaround without losing texture.

Clients include: Coffee Tales, Café Noir, Lavonne Café, Milano, Paper and Pie

Micro-breweries & bars

Bars are the showcase, so we make them eye-catching and ergonomic for tight spaces. Cocktail stations let the barman dispense with speed.

Equipment: Cold rooms, towers and taps, flash chillers, kegerators, cocktail stations, glass washers, wine chillers.

A complete solution for the hub-and-spoke model, so brands can scale to many outlets without a full kitchen at each one.
