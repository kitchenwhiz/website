Our story

From an SJP Road shop to living kitchens

KitchenWhiz is the commercial kitchen brand of Unique Steel Products, a Bengaluru family business now in its second generation. Read our story →

Factory, 20,000 sq ft showroom and warehouse together on one campus in Bommasandra.

Shabbir Madraswala started Trade Links Corporation in 1977 from a small shop on SJP Road, trading hardware, tools and safety products. Shireen Madraswala later took charge of sales and customer relationships, and built the trust the business still runs on.

Today a team of close to 100 people covers design, projects, sales, manufacturing and service. Our aim is simple: to stay at the forefront of commercial kitchens, and be an invaluable part of our clients' living kitchens.

  • 1977Trade Links CorporationA small trading shop on SJP Road, Bengaluru.
  • 1988Into manufacturingA move to an industrial shed in Bommasandra to build stainless-steel stoves, then commercial kitchen equipment. KitchenWhiz is born.
  • 2003The next generationHuzefa Madraswala joins with an industrial engineering background and brings in new systems. He leads the company today.
  • TodayFour brands and the UNOX LoungeKitchenWhiz, Barwhiz, HeatKO and USP, plus Bengaluru's UNOX Lounge, all under one roof.

Questions

Commercial kitchens: common questions

Where is KitchenWhiz located?

Our showroom, factory and warehouse are at 235/E Bommasandra Industrial Area, Phase 3, Bengaluru 560099. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:30 pm. Directions on Google Maps.

Do you manufacture commercial kitchen equipment or only supply it?

Both. We build custom stainless steel equipment in our own factory in Bommasandra, and supply 100+ Indian and international brands such as UNOX, Hoshizaki, Winterhalter and Trufrost.

Can you design the whole kitchen, not just supply equipment?

Yes. We consult, design the layout and MEP points, draw the line in 3D, fabricate, install, train your team and service the kitchen afterwards. How we plan a kitchen.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Restaurants, hotels and clubs, bakeries, pizzerias, cafés, bars and microbreweries, cloud kitchens, QSR chains, central kitchens and institutional kitchens such as hospitals.

How big is your showroom?

Our showroom in Bommasandra has been expanded to 20,000 sq ft. It has Indian and imported equipment for every kind of kitchen, a demo centre, the UNOX Lounge and the Barwhiz bar studio. Plan your visit.

Can I see and try equipment before buying?

Yes. Our 20,000 sq ft showroom has a demo centre and the UNOX Lounge, where chefs can cook on the equipment before they buy. Call ahead for a demo slot.

Which stainless steel grades do you use?

Custom equipment is built in SS202 or SS304 depending on the use and budget. Our budget USP Ecoline range uses SS430.
