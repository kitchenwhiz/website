About USP Ecoline

What we make

Ecoline ships flat-packed to cut transport costs. Allen-screw fittings make it simple to assemble, and to dismantle again if you move.

Tops and shelves are SS430 with stiffener supports, on 38 mm pipe legs with nylon bushes. Standard lengths are 900, 1150 and 1500 mm, 600 mm deep and 850 mm high.

Every item carries a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Ecoline is sold in standard sizes only; for custom sizes see KW Originals.

The USP Ecoline range

  • Work table with cross support
  • Work table with one undershelf
  • Work table with two undershelves
  • Options: 100 mm back splash, two detachable overhead shelves
  • SS overhead shelves (mount for HeatKO IR500 / IR800 heaters)
  • SS 5-tier storage rack (900 or 1150 mm)
  • Single burner range with T35 high-pressure burner
